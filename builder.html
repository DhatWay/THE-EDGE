How legs are chosen

From every game the pipeline rated (Full Slate and Totals), one leg per game, strongest first. Teasers move each line toward you by the teaser points and re-price it on the model's spread of outcomes — in the NFL, legs that cross both 3 and 7 (favorites of 7.5–8.5, dogs of 1.5–2.5 on a 6-point teaser) gain the most and are tagged. The combined chance treats legs as independent, so same-game legs are never mixed. A parlay or teaser compounds the book's margin; it's only worth it when the combined chance beats the payout's break-even.
