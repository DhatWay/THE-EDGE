HOW TO USE THE EDGE

What it does

Every game gets one chance to cover (or win, or go over/under), built in steps you can read on each pick: the market's chance, then team strength, then situations ("spots") with their tested records, then rest and injuries. If that chance beats the price, it's a bet, sized by how big the edge is and how proven the sport is. Nothing is certain — the goal is to be on the right side of the price more often than not.

1 · One-time setup

  1. Settings: Supabase URL and key, Odds API key, bankroll and unit size. Under Edge Settings tick your books in My Books; leave "Size bets by backtest proof", "Moneylines for MLB and NHL" and "Low-vig books as the benchmark" on. Decision mode: Math only (Claude reviews only when you ask) or AI assisted (Claude reviews every pick).
  2. Admin: paste your CollegeFootballData key (free — see "Getting the college key" below), then tap Check Sources.
  3. Supabase: deploy the edge-relay function once (Edge Functions → Deploy a new function → Via Editor → paste → Deploy). It carries NHL goalies and NFL efficiency.
  4. Slate Test: run each sport once (Season picker → last season). This teaches the system its weights, spot records and calibration.

2 · Game days

  1. Admin → Full Data Refresh (in-season sports selected). Scores, lines and box scores come in first, then ratings.
  2. Live Matchups → ↻ Refresh once. This is the only thing that uses Odds API credits; nothing refreshes on its own.
  3. ▶ Run Pipeline. The status line tells you how many picks were made and saved, and why the rest passed.
  4. Today's Picks: Picks = 1U/2U bets. Full Slate = every game rated, best value first. Totals = over/unders. My Lines = lines you entered from other books.
  5. Place bets with the bet buttons (sim or real portfolio). Remove a pick you don't want; undo a bet before kickoff.

3 · Reading a pick

  • Market: the price's own chance (50% on a spread at its line).
  • + Strength: how much the ranking disagrees with the line, times a weight the Slate Test learned. Early-season ratings count less.
  • + Spots: situations that fired. Unproven spots count about 1 point; proven ones up to about 3.7 each.
  • + Rest/injuries: a small, capped nudge.
  • Value tier / Bet size: the tier is how big the edge is at the price; the size also allows for early-season ratings and how proven the sport is.
  • Best number: the best line among your books and the model's chance there.

4 · Other books, totals and parlays

  • ＋ Line (on each game in Live Matchups): enter any book's spread or total and price. You get the model's chance there, break-even and a verdict; save it and it's graded at that line.
  • Parlay & Teaser Builder: pick 4–10 legs and parlay or teaser; it fills the ticket with the strongest rated legs from separate games and shows the combined chance, payout and value.

5 · Records and results

  • Pick History: Picks, Full Slate, Totals, My Lines and My Bets. My Bets shows what you wagered, your profit and your balance. Finished games grade when you open the page (or tap ↻ Grade finished games).
  • Slate Test (once a month per sport): the honest scoreboard — profit at your bet amount, how the model's chances held up, spot records, and the Prop Check.

Getting the college key

  1. Open collegefootballdata.com and find the API key page (it's under "API" — the page that asks for your email to send a key).
  2. Enter your email and submit. The key arrives by email within a few minutes (check spam).
  3. In THE EDGE: Admin → CollegeFootballData key, paste it, Save, then tap Check Sources. It should say reachable.

The free tier allows about 1,000 calls a month; the app makes one a day at most for ratings.

If something looks wrong

  • Results stay pending: Pick History → ↻ Grade finished games. It lists any game it couldn't grade and why.
  • Picks don't reach Today's Picks: check the status line after Run Pipeline — it says what was saved, or why not.
  • A sport is missing: it may have passed every game (see Full Slate), or it wasn't refreshed in Live Matchups.
  • After uploading new files: Purge Cache, then reopen the app.
